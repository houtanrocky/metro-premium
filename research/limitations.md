# Limitations

1. **No empirical metro premium yet.** The export lacks an explicit verified price-total/unit mapping. Source monetary denomination is unresolved. The initial app deliberately contains no real price/m² results or regression estimates.
2. **Historical inconsistency.** The dataset card describes six months of 2024; the observed source includes other dates. The primary single-month rule is transparent but does not make the selected month representative.
3. **A later map is not a historical network.** OSM was retrieved in October 2026. Station opening dates and service status have not been reconstructed for December 2024. No assertion about 2024 accessibility is supported by this extract alone.
4. **Geographic validation remains partial.** Seven OSM relations and their members are acquired, not independently approved metro routes. Shared/proximate points are not inferred transfers. The missing reference image was not reconstructed. Only a small real walking area is tested; full-Tehran routes are absent.
5. **Mapped walking is imperfect.** Access tags, conditional restrictions, barriers, stairs, crossing legality and physical entrance attachment may be incomplete. Nearest-node straight connectors are modeled assumptions. A shortest path within a truncated graph can overestimate a route that exits the rectangle. Five test paths are software checks, not field validation of pedestrian access.
6. **Location uncertainty is central.** Most reported radii are 500 m; many radii are missing. Anonymized centers are not precise addresses. Radius zero does not prove exactness. Uniform-disk simulation is a declared scenario, not a source-provided distribution. Network distance is discontinuous at barriers and component boundaries.
7. **Market measurement is selective.** Advertisements are not transactions; repeated listings, agencies, bargaining and withdrawal can alter the sample. No unique property identifier exists. Similar attributes can belong to different units, and different attributes can describe the same unit.
8. **Controls are incomplete.** Amenities, floor and year have missing/censored information. School quality, noise, employment access, neighborhood reputation, legal status and other confounders are not fully measured. Neighborhood effects cannot prove causal identification.
9. **Inference is conditional.** Clustered errors require enough clusters and appropriate dependence assumptions. Cross-boundary spatial dependence and measurement error may remain. Diagnostic flags are not automatic corrections. A synthetic coefficient-recovery test validates code under a known generative model, not empirical assumptions.
10. **Aggregation trades utility for privacy.** n≥30 suppression reduces small-cell exposure but is not differential privacy or a formal reidentification guarantee. Do not expose arbitrary intersecting filters or individual point queries.
11. **No current housing advice.** December 2024 asking listings do not establish today's affordability, purchase suitability or a guaranteed return. No causal or peer-reviewed contribution is claimed by the educational website.

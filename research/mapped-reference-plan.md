# Historical mapped-reference extension, v0.2

Recorded 23 October 2026 before fitting any empirical accessibility-price model. This extends, rather than replaces, the primary statistical analysis plan. The complete housing audit and housing-only price summaries were already known.

The Overpass attic query requests the OSM database as recorded at 2024-12-01T00:00:00Z, the start of the selected listing month. Its `timestamp_osm_base` describes the responding server's base database, not the requested snapshot date. Archived mapping is not a reconstructed operating timetable. Unknown station opening dates and unmapped entrances still prevent the primary claim of nearest operational metro access.

Run a separately labeled exploratory analysis of shortest paths to a **retained subset of historically mapped entrances**. Retain entrances only with explicit historical OSM stop-area membership, a station/stop on a historically mapped Tehran metro route, exact orthographically normalized station identity on that line in the user-selected guide, valid coordinates and no mapped access prohibition. Do not assign stations by geographic proximity, infer transfers, invent missing entrances, or treat guide dates as opening dates. Document excluded identities, line coverage, ambiguous memberships and remaining omissions. Missing eligible entrances can overestimate distance. Report the outcome as nearest retained mapped entrance, not necessarily nearest operational metro entrance.

Acquire walking ways in the declared study rectangle with approximately 3 km extra geographic margin; use a conservative importer and retain disconnected components. Check edge lengths, access and foot-direction tags. No primary map-layer approval follows merely from acquisition. Node snapping (100 m baseline) and straight connectors are approximations, not independently certified routes. Flag a route as boundary-sensitive unless its measured length is below the Euclidean distance to the acquisition rectangle boundary: a shorter path leaving the rectangle is then impossible. This certifies truncation only under the modeled coordinate/length assumptions, not missing city connections or metro completeness.

Use coordinate-valid, known-radius records for the baseline exploratory models. Report coverage separately among all price-valid rows and among candidates, including snap/connection/boundary failures. Fit the three planned equations on common complete cases with the pre-existing adequacy gates. Retain extreme prices by default; compare deduplication, tail omission, 50/100/150 m snapping, radius subsets, and Euclidean distance. Use neighborhood-cluster covariance, exploratory residual spatial diagnostics and a 2 km spatial-cell bootstrap of the fixed-effects design (199 draws, with failed replicates reported). Bootstrap intervals remain a sensitivity exercise because approximate origins, arbitrary blocks and adjacent-block dependence are unresolved.

For uniform-area disk perturbations use 30 draws per candidate, explicitly as an assumed scenario, not a measured probability law. Report failure and distance-band instability. Refit models in 10 whole-sample perturbation draws; these ranges describe sensitivity, not confidence intervals or multiple-imputation inference. Radius-zero observations are not independently exact. No nominal-price pooling across years or fitted results selected for significance.

Keep the primary `findings_available` gate false until operational eligibility, completeness, representative real-route checks and the other primary conditions are met. Export aggregate exploratory diagnostics under a separate versioned field with its own scope and warnings; suppress groups smaller than 30. No owner input or additional permission is needed to execute this extension.

Technical source: [Overpass QL date setting](https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/Overpass_API/Overpass_QL#Date). The user-selected guide supports identities, not entrance coordinates or historical service dates.
